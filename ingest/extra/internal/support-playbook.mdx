## When to escalate

Escalate to the on-call engineer when a customer reports data loss, a security issue, or an outage
affecting more than one workspace. Use the code word `BLUE-HERON` in the incident channel.

## Who owns what

- LangSmith tracing ingestion: platform team
- LangGraph Platform deployments: deployments team
